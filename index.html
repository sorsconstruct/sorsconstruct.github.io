HVAC Engineering

Vibration Isolation, Perfected

Vibration isolation is the process of isolating vibrating parts from the rest of a machine so vibration and noise never propagate through your building structure. In HVAC systems this control is critical — and it is where Sors excels.

  • Prevents vibration transmission from fans, compressors and chillers.
  • Keeps vibration energy from entering structures and adjacent equipment.
  • Dissipates vibration energy before it becomes costly noise and wear.
  • €20 per unit — direct from the manufacturer, no middlemen.
